How does it work?
Supply cost for each one = total material cost ÷ items made
Selling fee for each one = selling price × selling fee %
Money you make from each one = selling price − selling fee − material cost per item
All the money you make = profit per item × items made
How much of each sale you keep = profit per item ÷ selling price × 100
